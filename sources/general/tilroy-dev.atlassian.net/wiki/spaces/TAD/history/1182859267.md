# Payment codes

Tilroy has a list of default payment types that can be used.

In addition it is possible to add specific payment types to your account.

Below list is a reference list of all payment type codes that could occur in the sales JSON.

The payment types for your account depend on the used payment provider and configuration of the account. It is limited to a subset of this list.
