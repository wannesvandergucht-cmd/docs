# Terminology

**Basket** = transaction = a shopping basket, online or in-store. When completed results in a sale and possibly one or more orders.

**Sale** = completed transaction = a financial transaction in the POS system that records turnover and payments.

**Order** = An order is used when there is no physical handover of goods or services at the time of transaction. The order registers that goods or services will be delivered later.

The registration of an order always happens in a sale. It could contain a down payment for the order but can also contain other over-the-counter products.

# Basket

The basket is what you work with in Tilroy before it becomes an actual sale. It allows you to fill the basket like you do on a shopping basket online.

Tilroy manages the prices and the promotions on the basket through our promotion engine.

The Basket API allows you to create baskets in Tilroy. It can be used for webshop integrations or custom implementations where you want to use the Tilroy engine to create baskets, orders and even sales in Tilroy from within your own system.

# Sale

The sale has a few important parts in its structure:

- Customer: if a customer is known on a sale it will include basic customer data

- Shop: details about the till, store and legal entity of the sale

- Lines: all product or order data within the sale

- Payments: contains the details of the payment types used in this transaction

The sale is created from the basket when it is finalized. It means the sale is final, registered in the POS and can’t be altered any more.

We only allow to link the customer to a sale afterwards e.g. if the cashier forgot to add the customer to the sale.

## Sale line types

We have several types of sale lines to consider 

- P = regular product line 

- R = return line 

- T = text line

- G = sale of a giftvoucher 

Sales turnover is determined by line types P and R.

Full details of the sales json can be found **here**.

# Order

Tilroy knows a few types of orders, but the most common are home delivery (HD) or click and collect (CC).

Orders can be placed online or an in-store order.

When an order is placed it is done through a sale, where the sale can hold a payment for an advance. The value of the advance could be a part or the complete order value. Defaults are configured in Tilroy.

When a home delivery is delivered it is also registered in a sale, the dispatch sale. When there are multiple deliveries for an order, it will result in multiple dispatch sales.

A click and collect order are collected in store and will be part of a sale. 
This sale can also contain over-the-counter products, other order collects and even newly created orders in store.

So for orders there are always multiple sales involved:

For Home Delivery:

- After payment online or in-store: the initial sale

- On dispatch: the dispatch sale

 - This could be more then one in case of partial shipments

For Click and Collect:

- After payment online or in-store: the initial sale

- On pickup in store: the pickup sale

 - On line level it refers to the order

 - This can be a combination with sale lines for

 - over-the-counter products

 - pick op of other CC orders

 - new CC or HD orders

### The order lines

When an order is placed the customer doesn’t receive any products or services from that order yet. As such it is not considered turnover nor does the stock change at this moment.

Therefore, you’ll not find lines of type P, but lines of type T containing order information. 
So a text line that has additional info on the order.

You can identify the lines based on:

- lines.type=T

- lines.collectMethod.isHomeDelivery=true or lines.collectMethod.isClickAndCollect=true

You can find the details about the order line in the lines.order segment. It contains the price, quantity and reference to the sku.

In the same segment payments you can find the means of payment that was used to pay for the advance. In this case "Bancontact/MC"

## The dispatch sale (home delivery)

For a home delivery the dispatch sale will always contain order lines that are dispatched for one specific order. A dispatch sale will not combine lines from separate orders.

You can identify the dispatch sale based on "isDispatch": true and orderNumber refers to the original order that is dispatched.

The lines segment contains the order lines that are dispatched. This time the lines are of type P as now it is turnover and stock will change. The field idTillBasketLineOrig": "280343493.1" refers to the original order line that is dispatched.
