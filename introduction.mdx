Tilroy is a cloud-based retail platform that acts as the central hub for your commerce operations. Its APIs let you sync products, prices, and stock from your ERP into Tilroy, export completed sales, transfers, and stock movements back to your back office, and interact in real time with customers, vouchers, and shopping baskets. Whether you are connecting an ERP system, building a webshop integration, or extending Tilroy's point-of-sale behavior, these APIs give you the building blocks you need.

## Key concepts

Tilroy organises its API surface into three patterns. Understanding which pattern applies to each operation is the foundation of every integration:

- **Import endpoints** (`/import/*`) — Send data into Tilroy asynchronously. Tilroy validates the payload on receipt and returns `202 Accepted`. Processing happens in a queue. You are responsible for handling validation errors and monitoring processing status.
- **Export endpoints** (`/export/*`) — Pull changes out of Tilroy in near real time. Responses are paginated, and messages are retained for 14 days. Use `dateExportedSince` to page through new events incrementally.
- **Synchronous APIs** — Interact with Tilroy directly in real time. Create or update a customer record, check a voucher balance, or read basket state. These calls return results immediately and are used where low latency matters.
