The Shop API gives you access to all cash-related events in your stores — money movements between cash locations, small petty cash transactions, and daily register counts. Use these endpoints to reconcile your register positions and financial flows in your ERP system.

## Money Transfers

**`GET /export/moneytransfer`**

Money transfers represent every cash movement that affects a register or cash location in your store. Use this endpoint to pull a complete picture of how cash flows between locations throughout the day.

Tilroy distinguishes two scenarios based on how the `from` and `to` fields are populated:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Transfer Between Locations" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Both `from` and `to` are set — cash moved from one location to another (for example, a register drop to the safe).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cash Position Change" icon="circle-dollar-to-slot">
    Only `from` or only `to` is set — a net change in cash position, such as a discrepancy recorded during a cash-up.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  Every petty cash event and every cash-up discrepancy also produces a corresponding money transfer record. You can rely solely on the money transfer endpoint for a unified view of all cash movements.
</Note>

## Petty Cash

**`GET /export/pettycashes`**

Petty cash records capture small in/out payments made directly from the register — for example, paying for a store supply or receiving a minor cash refund. Each petty cash event automatically produces a corresponding money transfer, so you have two options for tracking these transactions:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Via Petty Cash Endpoint">
    Pull from `/export/pettycashes` to get the full detail of each petty cash transaction, including the reason, amount, and register it originated from.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Via Money Transfers Only">
    If you only need the financial impact on cash positions, rely solely on `/export/moneytransfer`. Every petty cash event is already reflected there as a cash position change.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Cash-Ups

**`GET /export/cashups`**

Cash-ups are the register counts performed at the start or end of each business day. Tilroy records the counted amount alongside the expected amount, and any discrepancy automatically generates a money transfer to reflect the difference in your cash position.

<Tip>
  Pull cash-ups alongside money transfers during your end-of-day reconciliation process. The money transfer generated by a discrepancy will reference the cash-up so you can trace the source of any difference.
</Tip>

## Filtering and Pagination

All three endpoints support the same filtering and pagination parameters, consistent with other Tilroy export endpoints.

<ParamField query="dateExportedSince" type="string">
  Pull only records created or modified after this timestamp. Use this parameter to implement incremental polling and avoid re-processing records you have already received.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="count" type="integer">
  Number of records to return per page. Defaults to 10. Maximum value is 100.
</ParamField>

<Info>
  Export messages are retained for **14 days**. Ensure your polling interval stays well within this window to avoid missing records.
</Info>
